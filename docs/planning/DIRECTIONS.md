# Source direction semantics

The confirmed [content decisions](CONTENT-DECISIONS.md) supersede the earlier interpretation questions.

Uke faces Tori. A Success arrow indicates a successful throw/technique in that direction. Hiza-guruma's highlighted sectors identify regions where proper application can succeed. Preserve those diagrams and regions faithfully.

Screen coordinates in the inventory are layout evidence, not automatic anatomical kuzushi definitions. Do not collapse a successful-throw area into a single off-balancing vector. Preserve the original face-to-face diagram; do not invent a camera rotation or axis relabeling.

All original right-sided attack options and intentional loops are accepted for Phase 1. Their physical detail is taught through instruction and practice; the app's purpose is the big picture of continuous attack options.

Geometric mirror candidates remain non-publishable background preparation for deferred Phase 2. Reflecting coordinates alone does not establish left-handed execution, gripping, imagery or combination exceptions. There is no left-handed UI in Phase 1.
