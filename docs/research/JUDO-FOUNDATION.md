# Judo reference study — first pass, 8 November 2026

Purpose: improve technical understanding while modernizing the owner's original mind maps faithfully. This research does not authorize new techniques or changes to the teaching relationships.

## Sources consulted

- [Kodokan technique names and definitions](https://kdkjudo.org/en/techniques/names-of-judo-techniques/) and [official list PDF](https://kdkjudo.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Names-of-Judo-techniques-1.pdf): the published nomenclature covers 68 throwing and 32 grappling techniques. Read the textual definitions across the recognized categories; this is broader background than the application's original 40-throw Gokyo.
- [Kodokan definitions dated 1 October 2022](https://kdkjudo.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Kodokan-Definitions-of-Judo-Techniques-01.10.2022.pdf): reference for distinct technique identities and stated mechanics. Textual definition review does not establish every variation, combination or practical execution.
- [IJF classification](https://judo.ijf.org/classification): cross-reference, with source spelling/coverage differences retained rather than silently merged.
- [IJF Academy handbook, tachi-waza/ne-waza assessment sections](https://academy.ijf.org/student-handbook): technical study accounts for grips, movement, posture, repeated attempts, combinations, counters and transitions. Its example progression distinguishes same-technique continuation from combinations of different techniques. This supports treating repeated attacks as intentional, not graph errors.
- [IJF: The Need to Study Kata](https://www.ijf.org/news/show/the-need-to-study-kata): judo study includes form, principles, practice and discussion; a list of techniques alone is insufficient.
- [IJF technical analysis](https://www.ijf.org/news/show/ulaanbaatar-grand-slam-technical-analysis): examples include changing sides and continuing into groundwork. A reported competitive example does not prove that every source transition mirrors identically.

## Consequences for this project

The owner's Success indication denotes a successful throw in a direction/area. It is not to be automatically relabeled as kuzushi. Retain face-to-face Uke/Tori orientation, highlighted success sectors, attack continuations and repeat loops.

The owner's shodan background and reported sensei verification establish acceptance of the existing teaching content for this project. External references support implementation understanding; they do not replace those pedagogical decisions.

## Honest coverage

This is a first textual reference pass, not mastery of all judo or hand-to-hand combat. It does not claim instructor credentials, physical experience, review of all videos, access to every book, comprehensive historical/atemi/kata study, or validation of every possible variation.

Broader study and left-handed exceptions remain future work. Phase 1 stays focused on the unchanged original right-sided maps.
