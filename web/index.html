How to use the mind maps

Start by selecting your preferred perspective (Right or Left Handed) at the top left of the page. Right vs. Left handed reflect maps and attack directions across the vertical centerline. Twelve and six o’clock remain fixed; the other directional attacks exchange sides.

Choose All techniques, a Gokyo group, or a classification. The maps use your selected perspective, with Uke facing Tori.

A Success arrow or highlighted success area shows where that technique can succeed. If the attack does not succeed, select another surrounding attack and continue exploring. Repeating a technique or returning to an earlier one is intentional.

After a successful throw, continue toward Ne-waza. That part of the mind maps is not yet developed.

Scroll or swipe across a map, change its zoom, or choose Fit map. The list of directional attack options below provides the same attack links, including repeated placements; the map retains their spatial arrangement.
